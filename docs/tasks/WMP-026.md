## Context and objective

Persist catalog operations safely across restart/offline periods without
storing credentials or permitting one account/shop to drain another queue.

## Dependencies and files to read

- Catalog mutation/image clients, SessionStore, official Mini file/storage APIs,
  ADR-003 and WMP-017…WMP-020.

## In scope

- Bounded durable records, stable idempotency, FIFO dependencies, exponential
  retry with jitter, explicit terminal/conflict/auth/permission states and safe
  image local-path handling.

## Out of scope

- Tokens, signed URLs or large base64 blobs in storage; background infinite
  retry; silent conflict overwrite.

## Files potentially involved

- `miniprogram/lib/durable-outbox.ts`, app lifecycle integration, catalog/image
  controllers and focused tests.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Queue persists across close/reopen and is bounded by item and byte quotas.
- [x] Same logical attempt retains its idempotency key and entity ordering.
- [x] Account/shop generations isolate records and block revoked membership.
- [x] Independent operations progress around terminal/conflict items.
- [x] Persisted image paths are bounded and cleaned after apply/discard.
