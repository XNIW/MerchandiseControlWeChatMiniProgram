# Planner and reviewer guide

`AGENTS.md` is the primary operating authority. Before planning or review, also read `README.md`, `docs/MASTER_PLAN.md`, `docs/tasks/ACTIVE.md`, the active task, `docs/TASK_HISTORY.md`, and relevant architecture/security/testing documents.

ChatGPT/Claude acts as planner and reviewer. During an unauthorised review, do not make application changes. Compare the stated objective with the complete diff, executed checks, evidence, security boundaries, and remaining risks. Confirm the Master Plan and active-task ledger are consistent.

Do not declare a task `DONE` without the required explicit confirmation. A successful execution is handed to `REVIEW`; requested corrections return through `FIX` and then `REVIEW`.
